# Dead Flowers — image wish list

Photos we want for the site, where each one goes, and why. The look we're after: classic rock show, Colorado red rock, guitars, and roses. Dark, moody, warm stage light, so white and neon text can sit on top.

## How the photos will be used

| Use | Where on the site | Shape | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Page banners** | The strip at the top of About, Events, FAQ, and Contact (right now a purple sunburst) | Wide, about 3:1 (2400 × 800 px or bigger) | The page title sits on the left, so the subject should be on the right or spread out. We darken it so the title reads. |
| **Full-page backgrounds** | Behind a whole page, fixed while the page scrolls | Landscape, 16:10 (2560 × 1600 px or bigger) | Heavily darkened and blurred a little so the content on top stays readable. Needs a calm area, not lots of small detail. |
| **Button and tile images** | The Shop Rec / Shop Med cards and the three home page tiles (Merch, Events, The Shop) | About 4:3 (1200 × 900 px) | Shown small and darkened behind the button text, so one clear subject works best. |
| **Merch table backdrop** | Behind the shirts on the Merch page (right now a plain black curtain) | Wide, 16:9 (2560 × 1440 px) | Stays dark and out of focus behind the shirts. |
| **The merch table itself** | The table on the Merch page (right now drawn in code) | Wide, 16:9 (2560 × 1440 px) | A photo of a real table, straight on, with room above it for the sign. See item 9. |

## The list

### 1. Red Rocks view from the top row — full-page background
- **What:** looking down from the top seats over the stage, the rock walls on each side, and the plains or Denver lights beyond. Dusk or blue hour is best: glowing sky, stage lights on.
- **Where:** background for the Events page, and possibly the home page's "visit us" strip.
- **Why:** it's the most Colorado rock-and-roll view there is. It says "live music, here" before anyone reads a word, and it's local to a Palmer Lake shop.
- **Notes:** an empty venue at dusk works as well as a show. Keep the sky fairly plain at the top so text can sit there.

### 2. Red rock formations, close and dramatic — page banner
- **What:** red sandstone slabs against a deep blue sky, or lit warm at sunset. Wide crop.
- **Where:** banner on the About page ("Built loud. Tuned for the road.").
- **Why:** ties the shop to Colorado without needing the venue in frame. Garden of the Gods or Roxborough-style rock works too.

### 3. Crowd at a show — page banner and Events tile
- **What:** the crowd from behind, hands and lighters in the air, silhouetted against stage lights or haze.
- **Where:** banner on the Events page; image behind the "Events" tile on the home page.
- **Why:** the events page is about getting people to show up. A crowd says "this is the party."
- **Notes:** from behind or in silhouette, so no one is recognizable (see "Rights" below).

### 4. Stage covered in gear — Merch table backdrop and FAQ banner
- **What:** a stage before or after a show: tangled cables, mic stands, amp stacks, pedalboards, setlist taped to the floor, monitor wedges, road cases. Warm stage light, some haze.
- **Where:** behind the merch table on the Merch page (replacing the black curtain); banner on the FAQ page.
- **Why:** the merch page is built to feel like the merch table at a show, so the stage belongs right behind it. The FAQ is the "road crew" page, the practical stuff.

### 5. Guitars — Shop Rec / Shop Med buttons and Contact banner
- **What:** close-ups of guitars: a headstock and tuning pegs, a worn Telecaster or Les Paul body, a row of guitars on a wall, strings catching the light.
- **Where:** behind the Shop Rec and Shop Med cards on the home page (one guitar each); banner on the Contact page.
- **Why:** the logo is guitar headstocks shaped into a leaf, so guitars on the shop buttons connect the menu to the brand. Two different guitars give the two buttons their own identity: orange for Rec, green for Med.
- **Notes:** avoid visible brand names on the headstock if possible.

### 6. Rock and roll roses — accents and the Merch tile
- **What:** dark red or black roses, ideally a little wilted ("dead flowers"), laid on a guitar, a leather jacket, a speaker grille, or a black stage floor. A single rose on a mic stand would be great.
- **Where:** behind the "Speed & Supply" merch tile on the home page; small accents next to the monthly deals and on the age gate later if we want.
- **Why:** it's the name. Roses plus rock gear is the clearest picture of "Dead Flowers" there is.

### 7. Inside the shop — The Shop tile and About page
- **What:** more photos of the shop like the ones we have: the murals, the vinyl peace sign, the album-cover wall, the counter at night with the case lights on.
- **Where:** behind "The Shop" tile on the home page; more photos for the About page gallery.
- **Why:** the shop itself is the best advertisement. We already have three good photos; a few more angles (and one at night from outside, with the sign lit) would round it out.

### 8. Shirts, hung or worn — Merch page
- **What:** each shirt photographed flat or on a hanger against a plain dark background, front and back. Also one or two of someone wearing them at the shop.
- **Where:** replaces the drawings on the merch table and in the shirt close-up. Robbie can upload them from the admin page.
- **Why:** real photos sell shirts far better than drawings, and a shirt on a real hanger fits the merch table look.

### 9. A real merch table — Merch page
- **What:** an actual Dead Flowers merch table set up and photographed straight on: folding table with a black cloth and a Dead Flowers banner on the front, shirts hanging on a rack or pegboard behind it, folded stacks, stickers, and a cash box on top. Shoot it at the shop, or at an event or car meet, with warm light and a dark background. Take one shot with the shirts hung and one with the wall empty, so the site can hang its own shirts there.
- **Where:** the Merch page. It would replace the drawn table and curtain, with the site's shirts and price tags laid over it.
- **Why:** the page is built to look like a merch table. A photo of the shop's own table makes it real, and shows people what they'll find at events.
- **Notes:** shoot from about chest height, level with the table, and leave room above it for the "Merch" sign. Keep it uncluttered so the shirts stand out.

## Specs for whoever takes or buys them

- Biggest size available; JPG is fine. We'll crop, darken, and compress them for the web.
- Shoot wide and leave empty space around the subject so we can crop for phones and desktops.
- Dark and warm beats bright and clean. Avoid flat daylight, white backgrounds, and heavy filters.

## Rights (important for a business site)

- **Use our own photos or properly licensed ones.** Free sites like Unsplash and Pexels allow business use; paid stock (Adobe Stock, Shutterstock) is the safe bet for specific shots. Don't pull photos from Google Images or other venues' sites.
- **Red Rocks:** the venue is run by the City of Denver, which requires a permit for commercial photo shoots there. A licensed stock photo is the simpler route. The site shouldn't suggest Dead Flowers is connected to the venue.
- **People:** anyone recognizable in a photo used for advertising needs to sign a release. Crowds from behind or in silhouette avoid the problem.
- **Bands and brands:** avoid band names, logos, or a famous musician's face in the new photos (the murals in our own shop are fine). Keep guitar brand logos small or out of frame.
- **Cannabis ad rules:** Colorado limits how marijuana businesses advertise, including imagery that appeals to people under 21. Rock show crowds should clearly read as adults. Worth including in the compliance review on the to-do list.
