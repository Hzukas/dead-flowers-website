The problem

Every shelf sells the same joint

When the brand on your shelf is the brand on every shelf, the customer shops one thing: the number. Whoever posts the lowest price wins the sale, and the next store undercuts it the following week.

That is the price war. Nobody wins it. You just bleed slower or faster than the store down the road.

The joint everyone carries Price-shopped before they walk in

Same brand, same pack, four stores on the same map. The deal is the whole relationship.
